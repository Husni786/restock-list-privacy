1. Introduction

Restock List is a standalone, offline-first mobile application built for retail store owners, shopkeepers, and inventory managers. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting all information entered into the application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores, how it is kept on your device, which Android permissions the app may request, and the choices you have over your data. It applies to the Restock List Android application (com.restock.retailer).

2. Zero external data collection

Restock List does not collect, store, transmit, or sell personal identifiers or business data on any remote server. There are no user tracking mechanisms, no third-party behavioural analytics tools, and no advertising network SDKs bundled within this application.

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission. It cannot upload your inventory, supplier, profile, or purchase data to a server. When you choose to share an export or open WhatsApp, the receiving app handles that action under its own privacy policy.

3. Information stored locally on your device

All data created within Restock List is stored on your device. Working data lives in an internal, private Room SQLite database. When you create a backup, Restock List also writes a complete JSON copy to Download/Restock/Backups so it survives an app reinstall. This data may include:

  • Store profile: shop name, proprietor name, and optional contact email.
  • Inventory and restock items: item names, variants, pack sizes, quantities, units, wholesale prices, and discount percentages.
  • Wholesaler contacts: supplier business names, phone numbers, and optional address or delivery notes.
  • Purchase history: timestamped records of finalised restock orders and supplier unit-price changes.
  • Preferences: units, categories, currency, theme, display size, and optional reminder time.

None of this data is transmitted to external servers by Restock List. It remains under your control on your device and in the backup folder you choose to use.

4. Device permissions and system intents

Restock List requests only the permissions needed for its core features. Depending on your Android version and the features you use, these may include:

  • Phone / dialer: when you tap the Call button next to a wholesaler, the app opens your device dialer with the supplier's number pre-filled. Restock List does not place automated calls or read your call logs.
  • WhatsApp / message: when you tap the WhatsApp action, the app opens WhatsApp (or a browser) with a pre-filled order message. You choose whether to send it. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to that message.
  • File sharing and FileProvider: when you tap Export PDF or Export CSV, the app creates a temporary document in its cache and opens the Android Share Sheet. You choose the destination app. Backups do not use the Share Sheet; they are saved directly to Download/Restock/Backups.
  • Backup folder access: Restock List needs access to Download/Restock/Backups to save and restore backups. On Android 10 and older this uses the standard storage permission. On Android 11 and newer, new backups are written through Android's MediaStore without broad storage access. After a reinstall, Android requires a one-time "All files access" grant so Restock List can read the backups left by the previous install. The app only uses this access for its backup folder and does not scan, modify, or upload other files.
  • Notifications: on Android 13 and newer, notification permission is requested only when you enable the optional daily restock reminder. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device; no server push service is used and no inventory data is included in the notification.
  • Restart reminders: the app uses Android's boot-completed signal only to restore your chosen daily reminder after the device restarts. It does not read or transmit personal data.

5. Android system backup

Android includes an optional system backup service. If you have enabled backup in your device or Google account settings, the operating system may include Restock List's app data (such as its local database and preferences) in your device backup. This backup is performed by Android/Google under your account settings, not by Restock List, and the developer does not have access to it.

You can control or disable this in Android Settings under System > Backup (the exact path depends on your device). Restock List's own Backup & Restore feature writes a separate JSON backup to Download/Restock/Backups on your device.

6. Data security and storage isolation

Your database is protected by Android application sandboxing. Other installed applications on your device cannot access Restock List's private database files under standard operating system security controls.

Backup files are not encrypted: they are standard JSON documents in a public Downloads folder, so they can be read by anyone who can access that folder on your device. Keep your device locked and delete old backups you no longer need.

7. User rights and data deletion

You retain complete control over your business data:

  • Individual records: you can edit or delete any inventory item, wholesaler, or past purchase record instantly in the app.
  • Full data erasure: you can permanently erase all application data at any time by going to Android Settings > Apps > Restock List > Storage & cache > Clear storage, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Backups: Restock List saves a complete JSON backup to Download/Restock/Backups whenever you tap Backup Now. Backup files are not removed when you uninstall the app; delete them with a file manager if you no longer need them.

8. Children's privacy

Restock List is a professional retail inventory utility intended for retail store owners and business managers. It is not directed to children under the age of 13, and no information from children is knowingly solicited or retained.
